summary: >
  This project investigates loofah as a plant-based, biodegradable material for
  embodied ecological reflection. A material exploration and design space combine
  loofah with indicators for temperature, UV radiation, water and soil pH, iron,
  and soil moisture, while also using it as a substrate for plant growth. A hat
  and glove with environmental sensing and living microgreens support a 15-day
  autoethnographic inquiry into wearing environmental change and cultivating
  on-body ecological practices and care.
