summary: >
  Recy-ctronics explores how electronic devices can be designed for
  straightforward recycling without giving up varied physical forms. The
  project combines recyclable polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) with liquid metal and
  accessible fabrication methods to create sheet, foam, and tube-based
  electronics. A comparative life-cycle assessment examines the environmental
  impact of these approaches against conventional electronics.
