summary: >
  This project investigates how deeply integrated social AI may reshape both its
  users and the people around them. Through a three-phase design study with 24
  participants, the work surfaces tensions around weakened interpersonal
  connection, privacy harms for secondary users, and threats to personal agency
  and identity. It argues for a more relational and provocative design
  perspective that considers the long-term social consequences of human–AI
  assemblages.
