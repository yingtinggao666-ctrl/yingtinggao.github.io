summary: >
  BIOGEM is a fully biodegradable McKibben actuator with integrated sensing,
  made from tunable gelatin-based composites. Stiff gelatin forms the outer
  braid, stretchable gelatin forms the air chamber, and ionic gelatin enables
  resistive strain sensing. Mechanical, electrical, enzymatic, and backyard-soil
  studies demonstrate reliable actuation and a sustainable end of life, while
  interactive, edible, and soil-degradable prototypes illustrate applications
  across HCI and soft robotics.
