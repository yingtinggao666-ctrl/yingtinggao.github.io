summary: >
  3D Printing Soap treats soap as a customizable, biodegradable medium for
  digital fabrication and interaction design. The project introduces printable
  soap recipes with additives for color, scent, conductivity, and environmental
  responsiveness, alongside a low-cost printer and custom extruder designed for
  soap. Evaluations of biodegradability, water solubility, and reusability—and a
  series of expressive, functional artifacts—demonstrate soap’s potential as a
  sustainable and intentionally ephemeral design material.
